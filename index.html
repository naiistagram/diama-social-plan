The Real Challenge

Service Delivery is not Brand Recognition

DIAMA delivers exceptional protective security on the ground. Right now, the socials are a mixed feed, with no consistent posting across LinkedIn and Meta and no clear content strategy behind them. Left unaddressed, that gap risks drifting into a plain services catalogue, and the people who need to trust DIAMA aren't just buying a service, they're buying character.

Current State

Mixed feed, no consistent posting across LI+Meta

→

Target State

Story-led brand, services woven in

Event organisers, venue managers and corporate security leads aren't short on companies offering officers. What they can't easily find is a partner with real operational discipline behind it: veteran-trained, preparedness-led, and accountable. That identity already exists inside DIAMA. It's just not been the thing leading the content. Every piece of content in this plan flows from fixing that.

"Protect. Prepare. Train. Give Back.": the thread running through everything, not a one-off About Us post.

Foundational Framework

The AIDA Model

Every piece of content DIAMA puts out serves one of four psychological stages. This is why the content roadmap is structured the way it is.

A

Attention

Interrupt the scroll. Get noticed by people who've never encountered DIAMA.
DIAMA Example
Officer photography (Hi-Vis, Patrol, Corporate Concierge modes), live event coverage, brand visual identity
I

Interest

Earn curiosity once they've noticed you. Make them want to know more.
DIAMA Example
"Protect. Prepare. Train. Give Back." storytelling, veteran identity, Barney's Invictus 2027 journey
D

Desire

Make them want DIAMA specifically, not just "a security company."
DIAMA Example
Martyn's Law educational authority, Executive Protection repositioned as premium & discreet, partnership credibility
A

Action

Give them an easy, low-friction next step when they're ready to act.
DIAMA Example
Enquiry CTA, DM prompt, consultation booking, website visit
This is the psychological backbone of the whole strategy. The TOFU / MOFU / BOFU funnel below applies this same model in practical, day-to-day content-calendar language.
Social Media Funnel

TOFU / MOFU / BOFU

Three funnel stages, each with a distinct goal. The funnel narrows intentionally: most of the audience will only ever sit in TOFU/MOFU, and that's fine.

Top of Funnel

Awareness

Maps to A + I
Goal: get DIAMA known. Zero selling. Build the name and the story first.
  • Veteran identity & "Protect. Prepare. Train. Give Back."
  • Officer photography across all three uniform modes
  • Event coverage: Leyton Orient, Windsor Racecourse, RM Rugby
  • Barney's Invictus 2027 story, human not corporate
Middle of Funnel

Consideration

Maps to I + D
Goal: build trust. Show credibility and why DIAMA is different.
  • Martyn's Law: Vulnerability Assessments
  • Behavioural Detection Awareness Training
  • Emergency Procedures Training explainers
  • Executive Protection repositioning (premium, discreet)
Bottom of Funnel

Decision

Maps to D + A
Goal: convert the warm, already-aware audience into enquiries.
  • Direct service CTAs
  • "Get in touch" / consultation prompts
  • Testimonials & credibility proof
  • Light promotional content
Ratio Guidance: Month 1
100% TOFU: pure visual brand-building, no promo
From Month 2 onward, target roughly 80% TOFU/MOFU, 20% BOFU. This isn't caution for caution's sake: a purely promotional feed doesn't build the audience that makes promotion worth anything.
The Playbook

Content Roadmap

A phased approach that builds trust before it builds pipeline. Each month has a clear intent and a deliberate tone.

MONTH
1

Foundation

Premium Visual Reset

  • Brand positioning reflected perfectly: Professional, Trusted, Reliable, Intelligence-led, Premium
  • One consistent visual system: fixed crop ratios, template family, no mismatched graphics fighting each other on the grid
  • Design lifted to match the Canary Wharf presence, premium and aesthetically sharp, not ad-hoc
  • Officer photography rollout: Hi-Vis Event, Casual Patrol, Corporate Concierge modes
  • Retire the old feed habits: threat alerts, recruitment flyers, training certs and event snaps posted loose with no system
  • Confirmed kill: "felt, not seen" copy line, flagged and pulled by leadership, contradicts DIAMA officers needing to be visible
  • Zero sales content, the story and the look lead, services follow later
100% TOFU

80% TOFU/MOFU · 20% BOFU

Visual Identity

Officer Photography: Three Modes

Confirmed uniform modes for consistent, rotating officer imagery across the visual brand-building phase.

Mode 1

Hi-Vis Event / Front Gate

White shirt, black tie, two-tone or red hi-vis gilet, DIAMA diamond logo patch. Front-of-house, event and gate presence.

Mode 2

Casual Patrol / Stewarding

Fitted black polo, cargo trousers, boots: rugged, hands-on, physical. SIA Approved Contractor badge visible.

Mode 3

Corporate Concierge

Tailored black suit or blazer, lanyard, diamond pin. Premium, composed, sentry-style: male and female variants (hijab).

Beyond Social

The Next Layer: A Full Marketing System

A social content plan gets DIAMA seen. A full funnel system turns that attention into enquiries and keeps warm leads warm. Recommended for a future phase, once the brand-building months are live.

Lead Magnet

A downloadable asset, for example a Martyn's Law readiness checklist or venue vulnerability self-assessment, built directly from the educational carousel content already planned.

Landing Pages

Dedicated, service-specific pages (Executive Protection, Martyn's Law Compliance) that capture enquiries from social traffic instead of dropping people on a generic homepage.

Email Marketing

A simple nurture sequence off the back of the lead magnet, keeping DIAMA front of mind between the first click and the eventual enquiry.

Renurturing / Retargeting Strategy

Bringing back warm audiences who've engaged but not converted, so the funnel doesn't leak everyone who wasn't ready to act on day one.
